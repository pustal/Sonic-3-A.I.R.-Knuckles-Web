ROM

Sonic 3 A.I.R. needs your own copy of Sonic 3 & Knuckles: the 4 MB ROM of Sonic & Knuckles locked on to Sonic 3, as a .bin or .md file with any name (Steam and the Sega Mega Drive & Genesis Classics include it as Sonic_Knuckles_wSonic3.bin). The TV checks it is that exact ROM. It can stay on a USB drive, in a folder named Sonic 3 AIR; a copy kept on the TV is used first, so only one copy is kept. A ROM with "sonic" in its name in RetroArch's roms/megadrive or roms/genesis folder on a USB drive is found and copied to the TV by itself, including Sonic & Knuckles and Sonic 3 as two 2 MB files.

Remastered soundtrack (optional)

A.I.R.'s remastered music. Without it the game plays the original Mega Drive soundtrack. Upload audioremaster.bin (about 126 MB) from the data folder of the official Sonic 3 A.I.R. download (sonic3air.org), then choose it in the game's Options, Audio. Like the ROM, it can stay on a USB drive in the Sonic 3 AIR folder.

Mods

Sonic 3 A.I.R. mods as .zip files, as they are published (for example on GameBanana). Turn them on and order them in the game's Mods menu. Mods on a USB drive go in Sonic 3 AIR/mods and show up in the game next to the TV's (moving a mod turns it off).

Saves

Save slots, unlocked secrets and achievements, save states, options and controls. A backup is a small zip file: keep it, or restore it on another TV. Restoring while the game runs takes effect when it starts again.

Trouble?

The game crashed at the last launch: its logs are the "last launch" ones below. The TV starts the game again when OK is pressed, or after 30 seconds.

The logs help when reporting a problem. This launch: the game's log, the app's start-up log. Last launch: the game's log, the app's start-up log.
